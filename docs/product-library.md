## TL;DR: The Spicy Truth

1. **We built a Ferrari and only sell rides at $497+/month.** HerEventTech is a premium program for female event tech *founders*. That's a small, high-ticket market. Great margins, low volume, slow to reach $10k.
2. **The volume is with young event marketers.** Coordinators, marketing managers, agency juniors, freelance event pros, people 22–32 who are good at the job, underpaid, and searching TikTok and LinkedIn at 11pm for templates, prompts and career moves.
3. **Most of what we built can be repackaged for them.** The GTM Playbook, Community-Led Growth, Viral Loops, Pricing Strategy and the quiz funnel all translate directly.
4. **To reach $10k/month, build a value ladder:** $27 impulse buy → $97 kit → $39/mo community → $697 cohort → HerEventTech coaching at the top. Details are in [Section 4](#4-the-10kmonth-math).

### Conservative path (months 4–6)

| Revenue stream | Volume | Price | Monthly |

| HerEventTech Founder tier | 2 members | $497 | **$994** |
| **Total** | | | **≈ $12,474/mo** |

### What feeds that machine (funnel math)

- **Traffic:** ~6,000 profile/landing visits/month from daily short-form video + 3x/week LinkedIn.
- **Quiz:** 25% start the Archetype Quiz = 1,500 → 50% give their email = **750 new leads/month**.
- **Tripwire:** 8% of leads buy E1 or a template = **60 sales**, plus another ~30 from direct link-in-bio traffic.
- **Green Room:** 10% of buyers start a trial and 60% convert, so the community grows by ~5–6 net members a month from buyers alone, plus direct joins. Expect 75 members by month 5–6.
- **Cohort:** 2% of the email list per launch.

### Stretch path ($20k+)
Add **1 Team License a month ($3,500)**, scale the Green Room to 200 members ($7,800), and move 3–5 Green Room alumni up into HerEventTech each quarter.
